Our story

Meet Leah & João

Coco Loco opened in early 2024, run by Leah and João, an English-Portuguese couple who wanted the kind of bar they'd love to find on holiday: great cocktails, friendly faces and something going on every night.

Upstairs it's tiki torches and fresh fruit. Downstairs it's cowboy hats and the match on. Families, couples, hen parties and wedding crews all end up at the same bar by the end of the night.
